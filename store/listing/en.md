## Summary (Firefox AMO: max 250 characters)

A private start page for your new tab: bookmarks in groups, notes with Markdown, tasks with deadlines, clocks with world time zones and web search. Everything stays in your browser. No account, no cloud, no ads, no tracking.

## Detailed description

FluxHome replaces your new tab page with a fast, customizable start page. Everything you add is stored only in your browser: there is no account, no cloud, no ads and no telemetry.

WHAT YOU GET
• Bookmarks in groups (tabs). Drag to reorder bookmarks, groups and cards. Optional site icons.
• Notes with Markdown: headings, lists, bold, italic, code and links. Pin the important ones.
• Tasks with priorities, deadlines and optional times. Overdue tasks get a warning triangle.
• Clock: digital or analog, plus world clocks with a list of time zones.
• Instant search through your bookmarks (press "/") and a web search bar with Brave Search, DuckDuckGo or Google.
• Right-click any link or page and choose "Save to FluxHome". The toolbar button saves the current page.
• Themes (light, dark, automatic), ready-made backgrounds or your own image, 1 to 4 columns, transparent "glass" cards.
• English and Russian. More languages can be added by translators.
• Export and import your data as a JSON file, so you can back up or move to another browser.

PRIVACY FIRST
• Data never leaves your device unless you export it yourself.
• Minimal permissions. Access to websites is requested only if you turn on site icons.
• Open source (MIT license): https://github.com/agteamdev/fluxhome
